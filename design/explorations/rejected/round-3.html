Loom, round 3: simple, with threads

A plain, quiet chat app first: near-monochrome, one centred column, replies as text, one big composer. Loom shows up in one place only: every agent is a thread.

  • An agent is a thread in its own colour, and the only colour in the app. In the sidebar it lies still while the agent is idle and ripples while it works.
  • A reply hangs on its agent's thread. The thread moves down the side of the reply while it's written, settles into a straight line when it's done, and ties a knot where the agent stops to ask you.
  • A new chat starts with your agents' threads woven into the loom mark, and you pick who you're talking to.
