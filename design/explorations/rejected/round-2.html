Loom, round 2: four directions that start from the loom

Round one kept the standard chat app (sidebar, bubbles, composer) and changed its paint. This round starts from the other end. Each direction takes one real thing from weaving, makes it the way the whole window is organised, and then checks that the same conversation still reads well in it. The content is identical in all four, so they compare. Everything is drawn with what GPUI has: fills, lines, paths, gradients and text. No blur, no rotated text, nothing floating over a panel.
