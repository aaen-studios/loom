One agent hands work to another

When Main asks Builder for help (message_agent), a short strand of Main's colour runs down the edge of the list to Builder. Builder wears that strand, wound round its own thread, for as long as it works for Main, and sends a strand of its own back up with the answer. Nothing crosses the text.

More ideas to play with

Pull to pin. Drag an agent by its thread to the top of the sidebar to pin it. Let go and it springs into place.

Speed is real. A writing thread ripples at the speed tokens arrive, so a slow model looks slow and a fast one hums.

Done is a snap, then idle. A run finishing is a one-time animation, not a state: the thread pulls taut with a small overshoot and goes back to idle. A reply you haven't read yet could be shown by the agent's row in bold instead.

Knots you can feel. A waiting thread gives a little tug every few seconds, harder the longer it waits.

Frays, then mends. A failed agent's thread frays; fix it (log in, install the program) and the strands twist back together.

Routines doze. An agent waiting for its 07:00 routine hangs slack and sways slowly, then wakes and straightens a minute before.

Your messages, too. Sending a message gives the agent's thread a quick tug, as if you pulled on it to get its attention.

Calm when you want it. "Reduce motion" keeps the shapes (knot, frayed end, dots) and drops the movement.
