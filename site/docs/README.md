# Loom's docs

Loom is an app for agents that work for you: they answer, remember, run routines on a schedule, and ask before they do anything that matters. The instance they live on runs on your server or on your PC, and you reach it from the desktop app and your phone.

- [Running loom](running.md): on a server, in Docker, or on your PC; backups and moving.
- [Agents and keys](agents.md): agent programs, API keys, MCP servers and secrets.
- [People](people.md): inviting others to your instance, and managing them.
- [Phones](phones.md): adding your phone.
- [Tailscale](tailscale.md): reaching loom from anywhere without opening it to the internet.

How the apps talk to an instance is in [the protocol](../../protocol/protocol.md).
