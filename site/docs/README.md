# Loom's docs

Loom is an app for agents that work for you: they answer, remember, run routines on a schedule, and ask before they do anything that matters. The instance they live on runs on your server or on your PC, and you reach it from the desktop app and your phone.

## Setting up

- [Running loom](running.md): on a server, in Docker, or on your PC; backups and moving.
- [Agents and keys](agents.md): agent programs, API keys, making an agent, agent defaults, MCP servers and secrets.
- [People](people.md): inviting others to your instance, and managing them.
- [Adding a device](phones.md): adding your phone or another computer with a code, and your devices.
- [Building the iPhone app](iphone.md): on a Mac, for the simulator or your own phone.
- [Tailscale](tailscale.md): reaching loom from anywhere without opening it to the internet.

## Using loom

- [Settings](settings.md): the settings popup, and how this device shows loom: appearance, the chat, notifications and updates.
- [In the chat](chat.md): the sidebar's groups and folders, cards in replies, pictures, files and links in the message box, how agents use their tools in Code Mode, messages while an agent answers, a backup program, and moving a chat to another program.
- [The agent's browser](browser.md): watching it, taking over, teaching it a task, and what agents propose.
- [Permissions, rules and sandboxes](permissions.md): permission modes, rules with a what-if, and isolated agents with a network of their own.
- [This PC and your servers](devices.md): letting agents use this computer, and run commands on servers you own, asking you first.

How the apps talk to an instance is in [the protocol](../../protocol/protocol.md).
